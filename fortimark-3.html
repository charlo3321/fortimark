Why It Matters
Laser Engraving vs Sticker Labels

Not all asset marking is equal. Here's why laser engraving leaves traditional stickers and labels nowhere to hide.

Feature ⚡ Laser Engraving 🏷️ Sticker / Label
Permanence Burned directly into the material — lasts the lifetime of the asset Peels, fades, or wears off over time — especially outdoors
Tamper Resistance Cannot be removed without visibly damaging the asset — instant evidence of tampering Peeled off in seconds with no trace — zero deterrence once removed
Weather & Environment Unaffected by heat, cold, moisture, UV, chemicals, or abrasion Degrades rapidly in outdoor, industrial, or wet environments
Theft Deterrence Visible, irremovable markings signal that the asset is permanently identifiable Easily removed — thieves know a sticker is no real obstacle
Professionalism Clean, precise, factory-quality finish that enhances the look of your equipment Stickers look cheap, bubble, yellow, and peel at the edges over time
Surfaces Supported Works on metal, aluminium, stainless steel, plastic, and more Poor adhesion on rough or oily surfaces — often falls off
Maintenance Required None — mark once, protected forever Regular replacement needed as labels degrade or go missing
Proof of Ownership Engraved ID is undeniable — the asset speaks for itself A missing or swapped sticker provides no proof whatsoever
⚡
The Bottom Line

Stickers are temporary. Laser engraving is permanent. If you're serious about protecting your assets, the choice is clear — engraving is the only marking method that cannot be undone, ignored, or removed by a thief. FortiMark brings that level of protection directly to your door.
